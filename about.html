About IVSS

Keeping solar assets safe and productive.

Founded in December 2018, Infinity Vogt Solar Services focuses on the operation and maintenance of grid-connected solar power plants. Our work supports reliable energy generation and long-term asset care through technical quality, clear reporting, and coordinated field teams.

Our focus is to help solar facilities operate safely, consistently, and in line with their performance goals.

How we work

The principles behind our work.

Responsible solar operations depend on more than equipment. They require skilled people, clear processes, and a consistent focus on safety and performance.

Safety first

Promote safe work through site induction, training, risk monitoring, and permit coordination.

Technical quality

Support plant reliability through planned maintenance, specialist inspections, and careful follow-up.

Performance focus

Monitor operating data and work to sustain energy generation and expected performance.

Coordinated teamwork

Bring reporting, field teams, and technical activities together to support consistent operations.

Our capabilities

Practical support across the solar asset lifecycle.

From daily monitoring to electrical and mechanical maintenance, module cleaning, testing, thermography, and HSE reporting, our services address the needs of operating solar sites.
